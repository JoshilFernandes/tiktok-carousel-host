Privacy Policy

Carousel Drafts Helper is a personal automation tool operated by its owner for their own TikTok account.

The app uses TikTok Login Kit to obtain an access token for the owner's account and uses the Content Posting API to upload photo carousels to the owner's TikTok drafts. The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer.

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. No analytics or third-party trackers are used.
